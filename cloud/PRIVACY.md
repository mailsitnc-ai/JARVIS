# Privacy policy — JARVIS

JARVIS is a personal assistant built and run by one person, Shivam, for his own use. It is not a
product, it has no customers, and it is not offered to anyone else.

## Who can use it

Exactly one WhatsApp number — the owner's. Messages from any other number are ignored and never
processed, stored or answered.

## What it does with messages

When the owner sends a message, it is passed to a language model (Groq) to work out a reply, and the
reply is sent back over WhatsApp. The last few turns of the conversation are kept for up to 24 hours
so the assistant can follow what is being discussed, and are then deleted automatically. A request
that needs the owner's own computer is kept in a short list until that computer carries it out.

## What it does not do

Nothing is sold, shared, published or used for advertising, profiling or training. There are no
third-party trackers and no analytics. No one other than the owner can read any of it.

## Where it runs

On Cloudflare Workers, under the owner's own account, and on the owner's own Mac.

## Deleting your data

The only person whose data is here is the owner, who can erase all of it at any time by deleting the
storage namespace in his Cloudflare account. Everything else expires by itself within 24 hours.
